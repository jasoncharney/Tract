Hold the mouse down and move across the strip to speak, or hold a key and let it scan itself — release and the gate closes. Dwell on a phoneme and it holds. Stops get no block of their own: each is folded onto the front or the back of its neighbour, drawn in orange, and fires as you arrive at that block or leave it — so a steady scan never waits in silence for a consonant. Word gaps close the gate.

ASD hold to scan — slow (4–6 s per phoneme) · mid (0.5–1 s) · speaking (0.25–0.75 s). Let go and the gate closes where you are; press again to carry on from there.
WER one word at slow · mid · speaking tempo
Q a random phoneme from the phrase T a random stop or fricative, unvoiced — percussion
1–8 phrase · ↑ ↓ step through them · Home back to the start of the phrase

control from Max (OSC over UDP)

Run node scan-bridge/scan-bridge.js from the repo root, then send OSC to 127.0.0.1:7400 from [udpsend 127.0.0.1 7400]. 

/scan/position 3.42     scan position, in cells (index + fraction)
/scan/norm 0.5          same thing, normalised 0..1
/scan/index 3           jump to the centre of cell 3
/scan/gate 1            1 = sound, 0 = release
/scan/phrase 3          choose phrase 1-8
/scan/next              step to the next phrase
/scan/prev              step back
/scan/note 60.5         pitch as a MIDI note number (fractional ok)
/scan/bend -2.          semitone offset
/scan/glide 0.05        portamento seconds
/scan/text "hello"      set words outside the phrase list
/scan/phonemes "hɛloʊ"  set IPA directly
/scan/param burstTime 0.012
/scan/tract 44          tract length
/scan/whisper 0
/scan/gain 1.

Feedback comes back on 127.0.0.1:7401 (/scan/out/cell, /scan/out/gesture, /scan/out/phrase) — listen with [udpreceive 7401].
