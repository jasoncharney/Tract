The cue light

nothing No cue for you. Carry on.
amber, pulsing Soon. Your next instruction has been showing under next since the last downbeat — look up, not down.
4
counting Sometimes: a count-in. Go on the beat after 1.
a bright flash Go. The new instruction is now the one to follow.

The line under next is always there. Read it whenever you like; nothing changes until the flash.

A cue will always say which one it wants.

Two words - they mean different things

SCAN — hold a key and the playhead moves by itself at different rates: A slow, S middle, D fast. It keeps its place: let go, press again, and it carries on from where it stopped. Run off the end and the next press starts over.

SWEEP — hold the mouse down on the strip and move it yourself. Both left click and spacebar hold the phoneme you last hovered over.

The keys

ASD hold to scan — slow, middle, fast WER play one word — slow, middle, fast. Press again to start it over Q a voiced phoneme · T a noisy one Z whisper on / off · J change pitch space hold the sound on · esc stop at once 0 standby · 1–8 phrase · ↓ diminuendo

Getting on

  1. Join the wi-fi network the conductor names.
  2. Open a browser and type the address they give you — just the numbers, like 192.168.1.42:8080. Nothing before it, nothing after.
  3. Type your name, then choose your part. This laptop remembers both, so you only do it the first time — the conductor sees your name over your playhead.
  4. Click once anywhere on the page. No browser will make a sound until you do.
  5. Turn your volume up. Press 1, hold A for a moment — you should hear yourself — then press 0 for standby, which is silent.
  6. Now wait. Your part is named at the top of the page, and the cue light is on the left of your instruction.

If you are lost

  • Nothing coming out? Click once on the page — the browser will not make a sound until you do — then check your volume.
  • Sounding breathy when it should not? Whisper is still on from an earlier phrase. Z turns it off.
  • Held key doing nothing? You have run off the end of the phrase. Press it again.
  • Started a word at the wrong moment? esc stops anything that is running, at once.
  • Lost your place in the piece? The phrase number is at the top of your page, and the conductor's instruction is beside your cue light.
  • Never guess at a cue. If no instruction has arrived for you, carry on with what you are doing — that is the piece, not an oversight.
