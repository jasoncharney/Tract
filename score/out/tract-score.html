Cue sheet

How to read this

One page per phrase, cues in the order they are given. Each cue names the parts it speaks to; a part not named is sent nothing at all — no instruction and no light — and carries on with what it is already doing. The line at the right of a cue says who those silent parts are, so you can see at a glance that leaving them out was meant.

Under each phrase heading are the pitches that phrase gives each part, and how the pitch is decided there: this pitch, yours by seat, your choice, or a band to move about inside.

Nothing here is timed. When a cue lands is the conductor's decision, and the piece has no fixed length.

The four parts, over the whole piece

I E♭3 – A4
II B♭2 – B♭3
III D♭2 – A♭3
IV C1 – G3

Two players to a part at least. Extra players spread across the written pitches rather than doubling, so a part with three notes wants three players.

The cue sheet in these pages is printed from cues.json and parts.json — the files the players read live. Change a cue and run node score/make-score.js again rather than editing this document.
